# Code of conduct

changelog.earth welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone taking part deserves respectful treatment, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual orientation or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, especially with people who are new to the project.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Respect disagreement and decisions made by maintainers.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and stop behaviour when someone asks you to stop.
- Respect people's privacy and their right to decline further contact.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable. Neither are repeated disruption, spam or retaliation against someone for reporting a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents changelog.earth in public. Contributors and maintainers follow the same rules.

## Reporting a concern

Do not post sensitive reports or personal information in a public issue. On GitHub, use the offending content's menu to select **Report content**. Choose **Report to repository admins** if available, or **Report abuse to GitHub Support** for behaviour that violates GitHub's policies. See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for other reporting options, including reports about a maintainer.

Include links, relevant dates and a brief account of what happened. Share only the personal information needed to understand the report. Reports sent to GitHub Support go to GitHub, not directly to the project maintainer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review concerns they receive and consider the context, severity and any repeated behaviour. They may request a correction, issue a warning, hide or remove content, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Serious threats or harassment may lead to an immediate ban.

Maintainers will keep report details limited to people who need them to address the concern. A maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome. When no uninvolved maintainer is available, GitHub Support is an independent route for violations of GitHub's policies.
